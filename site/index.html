How it works
Three steps. Zero setup.
1

Download and run

Download the .exe and double-click it. No installation, no dependencies, no admin required.

2

Hover on your target

Move your cursor to whatever you want to click. Keep it still. The ring fills up as time passes.

3

Click fires automatically

When the ring fills, the click fires. Press F8 at any time to pause or resume from anywhere.

Features
Everything you need
🎯

Dwell Time

Adjust how long you need to hold still before a click fires. Range from 250ms to 2200ms.

⧉

Movement Tolerance

Set how much accidental jitter is ignored. Perfect for fine-tuned targets or loose hand control.

⏲

Click Cooldown

Prevent accidental double-fires. Set a minimum delay between consecutive clicks.

💻

Click Modes

Left click, right click, or double click. Switch modes directly from the panel — no setup needed.

🟢

Visual Ring Indicator

A progress ring follows your cursor showing exactly when the click will fire. Pro unlocks 5 extra colors and custom profiles.

📋

Custom Profiles (Pro)

Save and switch between multiple configurations. Perfect for swapping between games, apps, or workflows with a single click.

⌨

F8 Global Hotkey

Toggle on and off instantly from any app. No need to switch back to the panel. Works everywhere.

🗓

Always-On Cursor

Force the cursor to stay visible even on sites that hide it — handy for overlays and dark UIs.

💻

Panel Protection

The panel itself is excluded from clicking. Move your cursor back to dismiss or reconfigure without accidental fires.

🎮

Windows 11 Ready

Rounded corners, Mica backdrop, and high-DPI support. Looks and feels native on Win 10 and 11.
